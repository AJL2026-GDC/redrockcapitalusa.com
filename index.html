Approach

We add value in the ground, then share it with partners.

Most of the value in a development is created before the first building goes up: buying well, getting the entitlements and permits, and choosing the right exit. That is the work we do, and partners join one project or several.

How a project creates value

The sequence on every land position.

  1. Acquire below valueLand in growth corridors, bought off-market or through relationships.
  2. Entitle and permitZoning, site plans and permits that turn raw land into a buildable site.
  3. Choose the exitSell the entitled site, build it with a joint-venture partner, or seller-finance it.

How partners come in

The same three steps for every partner.

  1. Sign the NDATell us which project interests you and we send a mutual NDA.
  2. Review the project summaryA short brief on the project: size, valuation, structure options and timing.
  3. Enter the data roomDue-diligence files, then a call with our team. We review projects with partners every 15 days.
